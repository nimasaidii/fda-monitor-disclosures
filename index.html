Personal notification utility

FDA Food Inspection Monitor

A privately operated utility that checks official U.S. Food and Drug Administration food inspection and enforcement records and prepares company-specific change notifications for its owner.

The utility uses Gmail only to send these notifications to the owner's own email address after the owner authorizes it. It does not request permission to read or modify the owner's inbox.

This page describes the utility for Google OAuth consent. It does not provide a public sign-up service. The monitoring code, history, credentials, and email reports are kept in private storage.

Personal-use terms

This utility is available only to its owner. It does not offer public registration or a public monitoring service. The owner controls authorization, can revoke Gmail access, and can disable monitoring at any time. Reports depend on the availability and contents of official FDA sources; unavailable information is identified in the report.

This independent utility is not operated or endorsed by the FDA or Google.
